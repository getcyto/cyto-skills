# Contributing

A good skill here is small, earns its place, and comes from a real failure.

1. **Start from the failure.** Say what went wrong without the skill, in one
   or two sentences. A skill with no failure behind it is a style guide.
2. **One folder per skill**, at `skills/<name>/SKILL.md`, with `name` and a
   `description` that says when to use it, not just what it is.
3. **Keep it portable.** No private names, credentials, internal URLs or
   client details. Examples should be illustrative, not lifted from a real
   system.
4. **Show it working.** In the pull request, paste a short before and after
   from a real session.

Open an issue first for anything large.
